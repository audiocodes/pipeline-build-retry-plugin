Log patterns that mean a real bug, one per line, at most 20. When any of them is found, the build is not retried, whatever else says it should be: agent loss, the regular expression above, or the policy for aborted builds. A successful build, a user abort, a superseded build, a point of no return and a Replay keep their own reasons. In a Jenkinsfile: neverRetryPatterns: ['COMPILATION ERROR', 'error: cannot find symbol'].

Good patterns name an error that a rerun cannot fix: COMPILATION ERROR, error: cannot find symbol, or the text of the job's own error('…') for a bad parameter. Avoid catch-all text such as Maven's BUILD FAILURE: flaky failures print it too.

Each pattern is a Java regular expression, case-sensitive (start it with (?i) to ignore case), matched inside one line of the log, after secrets are masked. The plugin's own [Build Retry] lines are skipped. The patterns follow "Where to search": with failedStages only the failed stages' output is searched, and with no failed stage the whole log. They share the regular expression's timeout; a search that times out or cannot read the log does not stop the retry, and a slow regular expression above can leave a pattern unread. The console line says which pattern matched by its number, never its text, and gives the console line of the match when the whole log was searched.
